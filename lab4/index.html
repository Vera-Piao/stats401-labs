Lab 4 Assignment

Monthly Tweet Sentiment in 2017

Each bar shows the percentage of original tweets classified as negative, neutral, or positive during that month.

Short Analysis

Data, Method, and Findings

This visualization uses a public archive of Donald Trump’s tweets from 2017. The original file contained 2,605 records. I converted tweet IDs to strings, standardized dates and source names, checked engagement values, removed three records outside 2017, and excluded 310 retweets so that repeated content would not dominate the analysis. The final dataset contains 2,292 original tweets. Sentiment was estimated from the tweet text using the CardiffNLP Twitter RoBERTa model. For each tweet, the model produced negative, neutral, and positive probabilities, and the highest probability determined its category. These classifications are model-generated estimates rather than ground-truth labels. The normalized stacked bars show the monthly percentage of tweets in each sentiment category. Negative tweets made up the largest share in January and February, at approximately 45% and 46%. Positive tweets became more prevalent later in the year, reaching about 56% in September and 58% in December.
