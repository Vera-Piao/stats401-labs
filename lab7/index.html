Analysis

Temporal Network Observations

The following observations are based on changes visible across the 60-day animation.

  1. Overall connectivity: When moving the slider from the beginning toward the final days, the network generally becomes denser. More links appear at the same time, more companies become active, and the daily transaction total shown above the network is usually higher near the end. Days 45, 51, and 57 are especially dense, each showing 10 active relationships.
  2. Company activity: Fusion Electronics frequently appears as one of the largest nodes and connects with several different companies, particularly in the middle of the animation. In later frames, Ion Systems and Cedar Retail become noticeably larger and more connected. This indicates that their commercial activity becomes more important over time.
  3. Network clusters: Several early frames show small, separate pairs or groups rather than one large connected structure. This pattern is especially visible on earlier days. While later frames show larger connected groups, although some inactive or isolated companies remain visible.
  4. Changing relationships: Some links repeatedly disappear and return during the animation. Relationships such as Delta Components-Fusion Electronics and Apex Textiles-BlueRiver Logistics recur across the timeline. Other links are concentrated in particular periods. Fusion Electronics-Granite Wholesale is most visible earlier, while relationships such as Fusion Electronics-Ion Systems and Cedar Retail-Keystone Materials appear in the later frames. Changes in link width also show that transaction amounts vary when relationships reappear.
  5. Cross-regional activity: By checking company regions in the node tooltips and following the links across the animation, later frames appear to contain more relationships between companies from different regions. These later cross-regional links are also often relatively thick, suggesting that they represent a larger share of transaction value near the end of the period.
