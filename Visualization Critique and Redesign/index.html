Original Visualization and Source

Sports City

Created by Matt Chambers in 2018, the original Tableau visualization uses a four-petal flower for each city. Petal size encodes championship counts in MLB, NBA, NFL, and NHL, while the center can reveal the city total.

Source: Sports City on Tableau Public · author’s project page

Introduction to the Visualization and Data

What the visualization shows

Message

The visualization compares which North American metropolitan areas have won the most championships and shows how each total is divided among the four leagues.

Audience and tasks

It is designed for general sports fans who want to compare city totals, identify leaders, inspect league composition, and explore when individual titles occurred.

Data

The external CSV contains one championship event per row through 2018, with year, league, metropolitan area, and winning team. Titles are assigned to the metro where the team played when it won.
